Strictly OSINT & Public Accountability Notice
The Kenya Market Watch tracks anomalous trading volume and information asymmetry in listed equities on the Nairobi Securities Exchange (NSE). This system is engineered strictly for investigative journalism, anti-corruption monitoring, and citizen accountability under Article 35 of the Constitution of Kenya. It does NOT offer financial, trading, or investment advice, nor does it encourage market speculation.

Statutory Whistleblower & CMA Disclosure Protocol

Under Section 32B and Section 32D of the Capital Markets Act (Cap 485A of the Laws of Kenya), dealing in listed securities while in possession of non-public price-sensitive information constitutes insider trading, punishable by fines of up to five times the illegal profit realized and mandatory custodial sentences.
