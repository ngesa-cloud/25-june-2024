DOCUMENT REF: KAA/PPP/JKIA-EXP/2024-LEAK-01
SUBJECT: PRIVATELY INITIATED PROPOSAL (PIP) BY ADANI AIRPORTS HOLDINGS LIMITED (AAHL)
DATED: MARCH 2024 // SOURCE: INTERNAL WHISTLEBLOWER DISCLOSURE

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:
A 30-year concession proposed by Adani Airports Holdings Limited for the Build-Operate-Transfer (BOT) redevelopment of Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (JKIA), Nairobi. The proposal projects an initial capital investment of USD 1.85 Billion (approx. Ksh 238 Billion) in exchange for exclusive aeronautical and non-aeronautical revenue collection rights.

2. FLAGGED IRREGULARITIES & SOVEREIGN RISK FACTORS:
- Exemption from national public procurement competition (PPADA 2015, Section 43) via single-sourced Privately Initiated Proposal.
- Clause 14.2: Mandates Kenyan government sovereign guarantee against operational loss, requiring taxpayers to underwrite commercial debt.
- Clause 19.8: Tax holiday provisions and custom duty waivers exceeding statutory limits under the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) Act.
- Transfer of key national security infrastructure (Terminal 1A, 1B, 1C, Terminal 2, and new runway) to private foreign holding without parliamentary ratification under Article 118 of the Constitution of Kenya.

3. LEGAL INTERVENTION STATUS:
High Court Constitutional Petition E371 of 2024 (Okiya Omtatah & Law Society of Kenya v. KAA & Adani Group). Conservatory orders granted by Justice Chacha Mwita staying all implementation steps pending inter-partes determination.
