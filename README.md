## 📖 Overview

On Tuesday, 25 June 2024, tens of thousands of unarmed, mostly young Kenyans marched on Parliament Buildings in Nairobi to peacefully protest against the punitive tax proposals in the Finance Bill 2024. State security forces responded with live ammunition, snipers, tear gas, and kinetic violence, culminating in the historic breach of Parliament, dozens of casualties, and an enduring national reckoning.

This project serves as a permanent, open-access, and factual digital record of that pivotal day. It brings together verified forensic reporting, official records, civilian photographic evidence, an accountable memorial roll of confirmed victims, and the soundtrack of cultural resistance that fueled the Gen Z movement.

---

## 🔍 Core Features

- **Hour-by-Hour Chronological Spine:**  
  A verified sequence reconstructing events from early morning tear gas deployments to the breach of Parliament and midnight presidential address, synthesized from Reuters, BBC, Al Jazeera, CNN, and the Kenya National Commission on Human Rights (KNCHR).

- **Digital Memorial Roll & Accountability Index:**  
  Dignified remembrance dossiers of confirmed victims (including Rex Masai, Evans Kiratu, Kennedy Onyango, Eric Shieni, and others), documenting reported causes of death, forensic evidence, and official case status without sensationalism.

- **Verified Photographic & Social Evidence:**  
  Carefully curated and attributed imagery from photojournalists and citizens on the ground, including the iconic *"Rio de Kanairo"* demonstration atop the CBD traffic lights.

- **Movement Soundtrack & Archival Player:**  
  An integrated, persistent audio player showcasing the songs, chants, and spoken-word anthems created during the resistance (featuring Eric Wainaina, Bien, Octopizzo, G-Bluster, and others), engineered to dynamically shuffle as you scroll through the documentary.

- **Minimalist Editorial Typography & Fluid Design:**  
  Built strictly with pure vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript adhering to a disciplined archival design system:
  - Deep carbon background (`#12140F`) and raised paneling (`#181B14`)
  - Newsprint paper typography (`Newsreader` and `IBM Plex Sans`)
  - Subdued ochre and symbolic terracotta crimson accents (`#B4472F`)
  - Fluid-dynamic vector SVG numeral bleeding with reduced-motion accessibility

## 🛠️ Architecture & Local Development

This project requires **zero external build tools, NPM packages, or frameworks**. It is fully self-contained in a single lightweight HTML structure with bundled assets.

### Run Locally

1. **Clone the repository:**
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/ngesa-cloud/25-june-2024.git
   cd 25-june-2024
   ```

2. **Start a local HTTP server:**
   ```bash
   # Python 3
   python3 -m http.server 8000

   # Or using Node.js npx
   npx serve .
   ```

3. **Open in browser:**
   ```
   http://localhost:8000
   ```

## 📚 Primary Sources & Verification Standards

All statements, death tolls, and timeline timestamps are correlated across primary public sources:
- **Kenya National Commission on Human Rights (KNCHR):** July 2024 Investigative Statements and Toll Tallies
- **Independent Policing Oversight Authority (IPOA):** Active Case Tracking
- **Amnesty International Kenya & Law Society of Kenya (LSK):** Joint Situation Monitoring
- **International & Domestic Press Dispatches:** Reuters, BBC News Africa, Al Jazeera English, Daily Nation, The Standard

## ⚖️ Ethics & Attribution

- **Confidential Records Exclusion:** In accordance with strict ethical standards, this archive does not host confidential medical-legal autopsy certificates or raw traumatic imagery.
- **Fair Use & Public Interest:** Materials are compiled solely for non-commercial historical preservation, civic education, and human rights accountability under international fair use doctrines.
